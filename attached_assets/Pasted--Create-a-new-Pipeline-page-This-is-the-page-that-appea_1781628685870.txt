Middle section — Conversation log. Shows the deal history as a simple feed: date, what happened (e.g. 'Pitch sent to Sarah Chen', 'Follow-up sent', 'Brand replied'). For demo purposes hardcode 2-3 log entries per deal. Below the log show a textarea: 'Log an update' with a small 'Add note' button.
Right section — Tools panel. This is where all negotiation tools live, shown contextually based on stage.
