Section 3 — Before you sign: contract checklist
Subheading: "Where independent creators most commonly get burned. Know what to ask your lawyer about — this is not legal advice."
Five items in cards:

⚠️ Usage rights — Perpetuity clauses let brands use your content forever including in paid ads and billboards. Always limit to a defined term and charge more for extended use.
🔒 Exclusivity — Category exclusivity means you can't work with competitors for a set period. Make sure the duration and category are clearly defined.
📡 Whitelisting / paid amplification — If a brand wants to boost your post as a paid ad, that's a separate right. It should cost more.
📝 Content ownership — Who owns the content post-campaign? By default you do — but contracts can transfer this. Never sign away ownership without additional compensation.
💰 Kill fee — If a brand cancels after you've started creating, ask for 50% of the agreed rate if cancelled within 14 days of delivery.

Right sidebar (sticky): Five negotiation principles in a stacked list:

Never lead with your rate
Rates are not formulaic
Usage rights cost extra
If everyone says yes, raise it
Budget ceiling ≠ your value
